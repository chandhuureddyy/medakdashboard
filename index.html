Infrastructure — Roads, Rail, Dams, Power

Telangana Govt. / NHAI / Railways data
🛣

National Highways

NH-44 (Delhi–Bangalore) near district boundary. NH-765 corridor. Manoharabad NH connection improving logistics.

🛤

State Highways

SH-1, SH-2 connect Medak–Hyderabad, Medak–Sangareddy, Medak–Kamareddy, Narsapur–Patancheru.

🚧

PMGSY Roads

~800+ km PMGSY all-weather roads connecting most villages. MGNREGS road works ongoing at mandal level.

🚂 Railway — 2023 Update: Kothapalli–Manoharabad rail line section inaugurated 3 October 2023. Regular Secunderabad–Siddipet DEMU service operational. Full electrification announced. Key station: Manoharabad Railway Station (~30 km from Medak Town).

Education — Schools & Institutions

DISE / Samagra Shiksha · School Education Dept. Telangana
📚

Govt. Primary Schools

All villages have at least one GPS (Cl. 1–5). Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan managed. Mid-day meal provided.

Govt. High Schools

Every mandal HQ has GHS (6–10). Some upgraded to +2 level. TSRJC for post-class 10.

Residential Schools

TSRJC, JNV Navodaya, KGBV in several mandals. Social Welfare residential schools for SC/ST students.

CBSE/ICSE private schools growing near Narsapur–Hyderabad corridor. English-medium demand high.

Economy — Agriculture, Industry & Schemes

Agriculture Dept. · TSIIC · Rytu Bandhu data
🌾

Agriculture

~60% population dependent. Rytu Bandhu: ₹5,000/acre/season. Paddy, maize, cotton, jowar.

🏭

Industrial Belt

Patancheru–Narsapur: pharma, chemicals, plastics. TSIIC zones Gummadidala & Jinnaram. ~15,000+ jobs.

🐄

Dairy & Livestock

Vijaya Dairy network, MACS cooperatives. Sheep/goat rearing. Poultry sector growing near highways.

🚗

Migration Labour

Daily wages to Hyderabad (~90 km). Construction, domestic, factory jobs. MGNREGS rural fallback.
